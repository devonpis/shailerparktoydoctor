# YouTube release schedule

The calendar for repair videos whose release dates were suggested by the agent. Check it before suggesting a publish date for a new video, and add each new release here.

All times are **Brisbane time (AEST, UTC+10, no daylight saving)**.

---

## Pattern used so far

One repair per week:

| Slot | Day | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Main video | Sunday | 10:00 am |
| Short 1 | Same Sunday | 6:30 pm |
| Short 2 | Following Wednesday | 6:30 pm |
| Further Shorts | Every 3 days after that | 6:30 pm |

- The next repair starts on the first Sunday after the previous repair's last Short.
- A single video with no Shorts (such as the spider) can go on a free weekday at 6:30 pm, at least 1 day from any other release.
- Never two releases at the same time. Same day is only for a main video and its own Short 1.
- Upload as Private first and wait for YouTube's copyright check before scheduling.
- Once the owner's channel has enough data, use Studio's Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube" chart to adjust the times.

When suggesting a slot, give the date, the day and the time, and say which releases it follows.
