# Product research — Mr Jones custom iguana (0126)

## Character (film)

- **Mr. Jones** — iguana in *Toy Story of Terror!* (2013 TV special, Disney·Pixar).
- On-screen look: green lizard body, orange dorsal spikes, black claws, prominent eyes, long pink tongue.

## Licensed plush (reference — not this base toy)

| Source | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| [Dan the Pixar Fan — Mr. Jones plush](http://www.danthepixarfan.com/2015/04/toy-story-of-terror-mr-jones-plush.html) | ~12 in Just Play plush, Toys “R” Us era (~2015); soft body, stiffer crest/claws. |
| [Pixar Post — Angel Kitty & Mr. Jones](https://pixarpost.com/2015/03/angel-kitty-and-mr-jones-plush-toys.html) | Retail launch context for the special’s plush line. |
| [Toy Wiz listing](https://toywiz.com/disney-toy-story-of-terror-mr-jones-12-inch-plush/) | Disney / Toy Story brand; UPC **886144211614** on listing. |

This repair used a **generic green iguana retail plush** (orange factory spike strip) as the starting body, then **custom-built** Mr. Jones features in the workshop.
