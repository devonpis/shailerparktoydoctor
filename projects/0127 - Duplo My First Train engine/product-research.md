# Product research — Duplo My First Train engine (0127)

## Set

- **LEGO DUPLO My First Train**, set **10507-1** (2013).
- Battery steam-style locomotive: red body, blue cab, black boiler front with yellow lamp prints.
- The side number **4281** is printed on the cab, element **98456pb02** (“Train Cab / Tender Base … 4281 Locomotive Pattern”). It is the locomotive print on this engine, not a second set number.
- Three AA cells. Green start/stop button, black fuel button, auto-stop after about two minutes without play.
- DUPLO plastic track does not carry power (not a 9V train).
- The full set also has a passenger wagon, a small station, two figures, and a loop of track. This job is the motorized engine.

## What failed

A 9-tooth 0.45-module nylon pinion on the 2 mm motor shaft was split. Off load the engine still ran. With a carriage coupled, the split opened and the motor stalled. Replaced with a 0.4-module brass gear of similar tooth count and the same shaft hole.
