# Product research — Vintage black baby doll (0128)

## Identification

**Maker and date are not confirmed.** No mark is visible in the repair footage.

Visible features:

- Glossy black moulded head and body, with jointed arms on rounded shoulder ends.
- Sculpted, combed hair moulded into the head.
- Yellow-green eyes, painted yellow brows, red painted lips.
- Gold-tone hoop earrings through the moulded ears.
- Red fabric pants with white lace trim.

The body material is also unconfirmed. It behaves like a rigid plastic in the video, but it was not tested.

## Comparison leads (not a match)

- **Pedigree (England)** made black plastic dolls in the 1950s, some marked on the neck. Listed examples differ from this doll, for example in wigs and brown eyes.
- **Ratti (Italy)** made a 1950s doll with gold-tone hoop earrings, but it is not the same design.

To confirm the maker, check the back of the neck, the upper back, and under the arms for a mark. Record the doll's height too.

## What failed

The torso cracked at the shoulder socket, with a split running from the socket opening toward the neck.

## Repair shown in the video

1. Arm removed.
2. Fibreglass mesh cut and fitted inside the socket.
3. Clear two-part epoxy bonds the mesh across the inside of the crack.
4. Black-tinted epoxy fills the split from outside.
5. Flat file levels the repair and cleans the socket rim.
6. Arm pressed back in.
7. Black paint touch-up with a fine brush.
