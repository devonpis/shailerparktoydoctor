# Product research — Kiddie Katch-A-Bus (0129)

## Identification

From the original box (`WIP-017.jpeg`):

- **Name:** Kiddie Motorized Katch-A-Bus, "A Child Guidance Toy".
- **Maker line:** Manufactured in Japan for Child Guidance Products, Inc., Bronx, N.Y. 10472. Questor logo at bottom right.
- **Contents:** complete layout with 2 automatic bus stops and 8 passengers.
- **Power:** 1 C battery, not included.

## Date

**About 1970. No date is printed on the front of the box.**

- Questor formed its educational products business around 1969, including Child Guidance, so a Questor-marked box should be 1969 or later.
- Gabriel Industries acquired Questor in 1978 by one account. Sources disagree on this sale.
- Retrowaste lists Kiddie Katch-A-Bus under 1970.
- The YouTube uploads and burned-in captions say "1960s". This page uses "about 1970" because of the Questor mark. Owner to decide whether to align them.

## What failed

1. Multiple cracked gears in the gearbox, replaced.
2. Perished rubber passenger belt, fabricated by hand over multiple tries for the right tension.
3. Dock release button not working because of cracked rollers, glued back together.

Stopping at the dock is normal. A lever stops the bus and shifts power from the wheels to the belt.
