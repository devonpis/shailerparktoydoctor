# Product research — Tickle Me Elmo TMX (0130)

## Identification

- **Product:** T.M.X. Tickle Me Elmo, Fisher-Price / Mattel, Sesame Street character.
- **Model:** H9207. The Mattel instruction sheet carries part code `H9207-0920`.
- **Tickle spots:** chin, tummy and foot. The sheet says to tickle him three times in a row for maximum laughter and play.
- **Power:** 6 AA (LR6) batteries, in the feet. The on/off switch is under the right foot.

## Date

**Introduced in September 2006.** The owner's notes and the YouTube copy say 2005. The instruction sheet PDF was created in May 2006, and press coverage places the launch on 19 September 2006. A 2005 copyright mark on the toy could explain the difference, but this has not been checked. The page uses September 2006. Owner to decide whether to align the videos.

## What failed

1. Corroded battery contacts. Compartment replaced from a second Elmo.
2. Dead main board with corrosion around the microcontroller. Board transplanted, with more than 30 wires reconnected.
3. Split nylon motor pinion, 8-tooth, 0.6 module. Replaced with brass.
4. Replacement board cable order did not match the original rotation sensor. Wires traced and reconnected.
5. Rolled right and stuck during the third routine. Fixed with a fine-tuning adjustment on the back.
