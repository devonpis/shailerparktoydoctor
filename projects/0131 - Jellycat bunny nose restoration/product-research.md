# Product research — Jellycat bunny (0131)

## Identification

- **Brand:** Jellycat, confirmed by the woven "Jellycat London" label.
- **Origin:** the care label reads Made in China.
- **Likely range:** Bashful Bunny, based on the ears, body shape and face. The exact size and colour name are not confirmed from the labels.
- **Company:** launched in London in 1999, according to the Selfridges brand note.

## Sources

- [Jellycat — Bashful Beige Bunny](https://us.jellycat.com/bashful-beige-bunny/)
- [Selfridges — Jellycat Bashful Bunny medium](https://www.selfridges.com/SA/en/cat/jellycat-bashful-bunny-medium-soft-toy-31cm_R03698449/)

## What was repaired

1. Nose bitten off by a dog, with torn fur and backing fabric.
2. Old stuffing removed, followed by a deep clean.
3. Original eyes removed and later refitted.
4. New fabric rejected because it did not match the faded colour.
5. Fabric transplanted from the foot to the nose area. The feet are slightly smaller as a result.
6. Head reinforced with iron-on backing.
7. Separate new pink nose made and sewn on.
8. Restuffed and closed by hand.
