# Product research — Christmas elf (0132)

## Identification

- **Type:** poseable Christmas elf doll with a moulded plastic face and black fabric suit.
- **Brand and model:** not identified. No label is visible in the repair photos, so none is claimed.

## What was repaired

1. Original elf's body and outfit were badly worn.
2. Client supplied a matching replacement elf.
3. Original head separated from the old body.
4. Original head fitted and secured on the replacement body.
