# Product research — vintage jointed teddy bear (0133)

## Identification

- **Type:** vintage jointed teddy bear with internal disc joints at the head, arms and legs.
- **Maker and age:** not identified. No label was found, so none is claimed.

## What was repaired

1. Brittle fur fabric with tears and holes across the body.
2. Neck split open, with old stuffing exposed.
3. Bear disassembled at every joint and all old stuffing removed.
4. Body and limbs washed.
5. Fabric glue, fusing web and cotton backing applied to every piece.
6. Holes and tears stitched closed over the new backing.
7. Restuffed, rejoined and finished with a blue ribbon.
