# Product research — SD Nu Gundam kit (0134)

## Identification

- **Type:** Bandai SD (super deformed) RX-93 Nu Gundam plastic model kit. The kit manual seen in the video shows the Nu Gundam artwork.
- **Exact product line:** not confirmed from the video, so none is claimed.

## What was done

1. Straight build taken apart.
2. Parts cleaned up with a hobby knife.
3. Details hand-painted in black, yellow, red, green and pink.
4. Panel lines filled and wiped back.
5. Flat clear coat (Tamiya TS-80, as shown in the video).
6. Reassembled with the fin funnels, rifle, shield and beam saber.
